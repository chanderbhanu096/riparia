Which of these does it look most like?
These look similar and mean very different things. You are standing there and we are not.
I
Long green strands or hair-like mats
Filamentous algae
II
Blue-green, like spilled paint or scum on the surface
Cyanobacterial scum
III
Grey or dirty-white slimy strands coating the bed
Sewage fungus
Possible cyanobacterial bloom
Blooms can produce toxins that affect people and animals, and they can develop and disperse within days — so timing matters for a reviewer.
For you and anyone with you. Keep children and dogs out of the water and off the shoreline until this has been assessed, and do not let animals drink. Dogs have died after drinking at affected water.
