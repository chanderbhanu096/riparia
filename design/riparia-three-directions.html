Riparia — three design directions

The same phone screen in each: a citizen has reported green growth and is being asked which of three lookalikes it is. Identical copy, identical specimen drawings — design is the only variable. Scroll sideways to compare.

Every direction draws the three growths rather than describing them, because that is the actual problem: nobody can separate filamentous algae from a cyanobacterial bloom from sewage fungus by reading three sentences. The drawings are schematic aids, not identification plates — they have not been checked against real field examples.
